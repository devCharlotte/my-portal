# WINDOW media sources

WINDOW uses real aircraft-window footage from Wikimedia Commons. All clips listed below are used as ambient footage; they are **not live footage of the selected BIGONG route**. MAP remains the route-accurate view. The page requests 720p VP9 transcodes where available.

- `Takeoff From Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas.webm` — Thomas Farley — CC0 1.0 — takeoff / city / high-altitude variants
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Takeoff_From_Harry_Reid_International_Airport_in_Las_Vegas.webm
- `Takeoff From Oakland International Airport (OAK).webm` — Thomas Farley — CC0 1.0 — sunset takeoff / bay / coast variants
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Takeoff_From_Oakland_International_Airport_(OAK).webm
- `20230509 RCTP Taoyuan airport takeoff.webm` — own work — CC0 1.0 — coastal takeoff variant
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20230509_RCTP_Taoyuan_airport_takeoff.webm
- `Flight over clouds.webm` — L. Shyamal — CC0 1.0 — clouds / wing cruise
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flight_over_clouds.webm
- `View of Lombok Island from air, Flight IU 762, 4 July 2024.webm` — Jeromi Mikhael — CC0 1.0 — island / land cruise
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Lombok_Island_from_air,_Flight_IU_762,_4_July_2024.webm
- `Plane landing at Tangier airport over Sidi Kacem beach.webm` — Ideophagous — CC0 1.0 — coastal approach
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plane_landing_at_Tangier_airport_over_Sidi_Kacem_beach.webm
- `Flight landing.webm` — Knowledge and philosophy — CC0 1.0 — final approach / landing
  - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flight_landing.webm

Selection logic: takeoff clips vary by local light; cruise alternates roughly every two session-minutes using route-seeded rotation; land/ocean state influences the pool; approach/landing clips take over near the end.
